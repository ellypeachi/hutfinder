Second photo picker

Each hut's collage gets a second photo next to its main one: inside the hut, or the landscape around it. Click a photo to pick it, and click it again to undo. The first tile is the main photo the hut already has. Frames are cropped to 4:3, the way the collage will show them.
When you're done, click Download for Claude and attach the file in the chat. It holds your picks and the photos themselves.
